
STUDENT SUPPORT POLICY

1. FEE PAYMENT POLICY
Students must pay their fees according to the institute fee schedule.
Students should contact the accounts department if a payment fails.
If a payment portal fails, students should contact the accounts department for guidance.
The policy does not guarantee automatic extension of payment deadlines.

2. SCHOLARSHIP POLICY
Students can apply for scholarships according to institute eligibility rules.
Scholarship approval depends on verification by the institute.
Students can check their scholarship status through the student support system.

3. COURSE CHANGE POLICY
Students who want to change their course must submit a formal request.
Course changes require approval from the institute.

4. SUPPORT REQUEST POLICY
Students can create support requests for fee issues, scholarships and academic queries.
The institute support team will review the requests.

5. PRIVACY POLICY
Students can access only their own private student records.
Access to another student's private information is prohibited.
Sensitive account information such as passwords and OTPs must not be stored in long-term memory.
